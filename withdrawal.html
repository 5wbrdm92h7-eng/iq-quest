Purchases, support & withdrawal

NOT A FINAL WITHDRAWAL POLICY

There is no active real-money checkout. The contract type, final statutory cancellation notice and online withdrawal function have not yet been approved. Do not pay real SOL for IQ QUEST at this stage.

Current availability

Browser games and local quest drafting are free. The planned single publication offer is priced at USD 3.99 equivalent in SOL. Additional IQ Labs storage and Solana network fees would need to be clearly displayed before a future order. Neither Mainnet payment nor live Mainnet publication is enabled.

What a future order must explain

  • The exact publication service and when it is considered delivered.
  • The final total price, taxes, exchange-rate validity, who pays network and storage fees, and any extra charges.
  • Transaction failure, duplicate-payment prevention, refund and support procedures.
  • The applicable statutory right of withdrawal, the correct model instructions/form, and any conditions for early performance or loss of that right.
  • A properly labelled payment-obligation button and durable order confirmation.

Online withdrawal

German consumer law includes a website withdrawal function for relevant distance contracts concluded online. A working submission and immediate durable receipt confirmation must be designed with the future order backend, where applicable. There is deliberately no fake or non-working button on this draft page.

Do not assume that the irreversibility of a Solana transaction removes any statutory consumer right. The correct legal treatment depends on the actual contract and performance.
