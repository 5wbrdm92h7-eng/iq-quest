2. Hosting and technical requests

The website is currently delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub documents that visitor IP addresses are logged for security reasons when Pages sites are accessed. Review GitHub's privacy statement for its practices. This operator must still check its own responsibilities, retention periods and international transfers.

3. Local browser storage

IQ QUEST uses browser local storage for game progress and personal best times, quest drafts, previews, import backups, Devnet recovery references and future Mainnet signed-payment and IQ Labs-upload recovery references. The game can also save local UI statistics. These records remain on the device/browser until cleared or overwritten; IQ QUEST does not synchronise local drafts to the cloud by default.

Clearing this site's browser data removes locally stored drafts and results. It does not delete public blockchain transactions or quests already inscribed through IQ Labs. The purpose and legal classification of each stored item under the GDPR and Germany's TDDDG must be confirmed before launch.

4. Wallet and blockchain

You can play, build and preview without connecting a wallet. During an explicitly initiated Devnet test payment or publication, the selected wallet exposes its public address and requests signatures from the user. IQ QUEST does not ask for seed phrases or private keys. Devnet payment references, wallet addresses and uploaded quest content may become publicly visible on Solana or through relevant network services. Onchain data can be difficult or impossible to erase.

5. Third-party services and outgoing links

  • When you request an indicative SOL price, your browser contacts CoinGecko. No payable exchange-rate quote is issued.
  • Using a wallet or retrieving an onchain quest may load software from esm.sh and contact Solana RPC endpoints or the IQ Labs integration.
  • Links to X and ChatGPT open third-party services only when you choose to follow them. Their own privacy terms apply.

There is currently no live commercial payment server and no Cloudflare deployment. Any future paid Mainnet publishing would require independent payment and IQ Labs publication verification; the hosting and verification approach is not yet selected. Such a system could need to process the payer's public wallet address, order reference, exact 15-question draft (including the creator name, questions, answers and hints), quest fingerprint, quoted amount, payment signature and separate IQ Labs inscription signature. Content uploaded to Solana after explicit wallet approval could become permanently public. A transaction to the payout wallet alone does not purchase or publish a quest. Before enabling paid service, the final privacy notice must accurately name the chosen processors, legal bases, retention periods and deletion/contact procedures.

Third-party contacts can involve IP addresses and other technical request data. Provider configurations, data transfers and any embedded functionality must be audited before Mainnet launch.

6. Your rights and missing launch information

Where the GDPR applies, individuals may have rights including access, rectification, deletion, restriction, data portability and objection, subject to the applicable conditions. You may also contact a competent data protection authority.
