02 · CHATGPT QUEST BUILDER✧ AI ASSIST

Let ChatGPT fill your 15 clues

Existing example answers do not block AI import. Generate your 15 puzzles in ChatGPT, then replace all 15 editor fields in one step. No API key needed. This updated importer also fixes common iPhone copy errors such as an accidental backslash before a closing bracket.

New to ChatGPT? Start with this example

In the field “Your mystery theme” above, you can write just a short idea, for example:

An ancient jungle temple. Create 15 clever puzzles using secret symbols, hidden codes and mysterious doors. Medium difficulty. The last puzzle must combine earlier clues.

You do not need to write all 15 questions yourself. After choosing your difficulty:

  1. Tap “GENERATE 15 PUZZLES WITH CHATGPT · COPY PROMPT” below.
  2. Tap “OPEN CHATGPT”, paste the copied text into the chat and send it.
  3. ChatGPT creates the 15 questions, hints and answers as JSON. Copy its entire response.
  4. Return here. Tap “PASTE FROM CLIPBOARD” or paste into the box, then tap “REPLACE ALL 15 EDITOR QUESTIONS”. The 15 existing examples will be replaced.
  5. Check the answers and test your quest before sharing.
